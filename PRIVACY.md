# Pint Split Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Pint Split is a mobile timing game created by Dean Vayias. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information handled by the app

Pint Split stores game progress, settings, saved runs, and Daily Five results locally on your device. The developer does not receive this information.

If you choose to use leaderboard features, Pint Split uses Apple Game Center to submit scores and display leaderboard standings. Apple may process your Game Center player identifier, display name, and gameplay scores under Apple's own privacy policy. Pint Split does not operate a separate account system or developer-controlled leaderboard server.

## Tracking, advertising, and third parties

Pint Split does not contain advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking technology, or third-party marketing SDKs. The developer does not sell personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete the app or its data. Game Center information is managed through your Apple Account and Game Center settings.

## Children's privacy

Pint Split is not directed to children. Its App Store age rating reflects its fictional alcohol-related theme.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
